Q19: should every page have a search box at the top?

Both pictures show the Diary page with the current Round 4 design (green menu, table bands). The only difference is a thin bar across the top. Today, searching for a food starts on the Add food or Find a food pages. The question is whether a small “Search foods…” box should also sit at the top of every page.

What the user does

To find a food, press Add food (in the menu or at the top of the Diary). Choose the meal, then type in the search box on the Add food page. Nothing else on the screen asks for attention.

Pros and cons

  • Pro: simplest and cleanest; more room for the table; one way to do it.
  • Pro: no duplicate search box on the Add food and Find a food pages.
  • Con: from Home, Progress or Friends, logging a food takes an extra step (Add food, pick meal, then type).

What the user does

On any page, click the “Search foods…” box at the top (or press the / key), type a few letters, and a short list of matching foods appears right there. Press the round + to add one, or choose See all results to open the full Find a food page. On a phone the box becomes a magnifier icon that opens full-screen search.

Pros and cons

  • Pro: a food can be found and added from anywhere in two or three taps, without first going to Diary.
  • Pro: familiar pattern; the / shortcut is quick on a computer.
  • Con: a fixed 60 px bar on every page, so slightly less room for tables.
  • Con: a third way to start adding food (menu button, Diary button and search), and the box repeats on the Add food and Find a food pages unless we hide it there.

What the user sees

  1. Clicks the box: a green outline shows it is active.
  2. Types “chap”: three matching foods drop down, with a + on each and a meal choice (“Add to Lunch”).
  3. Presses + to add a food (a toast with Undo appears), or presses See all results for “chap”, which opens the Find a food page with “chap” already typed.
  4. Press Esc or click away to close the list.

Good to know

  • The list shows the best 3 to 5 matches only; the full list, filters and the Servings and Serving size controls are on Find a food.
  • Adding straight from this list uses 1 serving, like the + on the Add food page.
  • For screen readers the list is announced as “3 results” and works with the arrow keys and Enter.
